## project overview
This project is a simple business website for *Glamour Crown Hair*, a small business that sells stylish and high-quality hair products for women. The website is designed to promote products, provide information, and allow customers to make enquiries.

## website structure
about.html - it will have our story,our mission,our vision,our values and our team
contact.html -it will have our contact details and address
enquiry.html -have information about our assistance
index.html -it have business information
product.html -it have features of product and prices
services.html -it has our IT support and how we offer our service

## Technologies Used
 HTML (structure of the website)
 Basic CSS (for styling, if added)
 Optional JavaScript (for form validation)

 
##Purpose of the Website

 Promote Glamour Crown Hair products
 Attract customers through online presence
Provide easy communication with customers
Support business growth and marketing

## How to Use

1. Open index.html in a web browser.
2. Use the navigation menu to move between pages.
3. View products and services.
4. Use the enquiry form to send a message.
